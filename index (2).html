About BIT Bargaon

An Emerging Center of Technical & Managerial Excellence

Bargaon Institute of Technology (BIT), located at Bargaon, Sundargarh, Odisha, is an emerging institution of technical and management education established under the aegis of BIG (Badagan Institutional Group) and promoted by the Managing Committee of a unit of Sebati Devi Foundation.

BIT was founded with a dedicated vision to deliver high-quality, value-based, and career-centric education to rural, tribal, and aspiring youth across Sundargarh and western Odisha. We believe that talent knows no boundaries, and every young mind deserves access to world-class learning facilities and modern vocational pathways.

Our Vision

Strive to establish and develop the institution to excel in engineering, management, technology, and research to produce trained, skilled, and industry-ready manpower of the highest calibre, assimilating ethical values to serve regional and national socio-economic growth.

Our Mission

Academic Offerings

Programs Designed for Industry Success

BIT provides 5 specialized Diploma Engineering streams approved by AICTE & affiliated to SCTE&VT, alongside prestigious Full-Time MBA & BBA programs affiliated to BPUT Odisha.

Eligibility: Any Graduate (50%)

Duration 3 Years (Full-Time)
Intake 120 Seats

Intake 60 Seats

Admission Procedure & Scholarships 2026-27

Transparent, merit-based admission guidelines aligned with D.T.E&T Odisha (SAMS Portal) and BPUT norms.

How to Apply in 4 Simple Steps

Follow the streamlined process for direct institutional seats and SAMS Odisha counseling allotments.

1
Eligibility Check

Ensure qualification: 10th pass for 3-Year Diploma; 12th pass for BBA; Any Graduation for 2-Year MBA.

2
Register / Apply

Fill out the online admission form or register through D.T.E&T (SAMS Odisha) selecting BIT Bargaon.

3
Document Verification

Submit 10th/12th marksheets, conduct certificate, caste certificate (if applicable), and passport photos.

4
Confirm Admission

Pay nominal admission fees as per Govt. norms, receive student registration ID, and apply for scholarships.

Financial Assistance

State & National Scholarships

Students admitted to Bargaon Institute of Technology are eligible to apply for numerous financial support schemes provided by the Government of Odisha and Central Government:

  • PRERANA & Post-Matric Scholarship for SC/ST/OBC/SEBC students.
  • Odisha State Scholarship Portal covering tuition assistance for underprivileged students.
  • AICTE Pragati & Saksham Schemes for meritorious girl students and differently-abled learners.
  • Merit & Minority Scholarships for top scorers in qualifying examinations.

Statutory Approvals & Affiliations
