About BIT Bargaon

An Emerging Center of Technical & Managerial Excellence

Bargaon Institute of Technology (BIT), located at Bargaon, Sundargarh, Odisha, is an emerging institution of technical and management education established under the aegis of BIG (Badagan Institutional Group) and promoted by the Managing Committee of a unit of Sebati Devi Foundation.

BIT was founded with a dedicated vision to deliver high-quality, value-based, and career-centric education to rural, tribal, and aspiring youth across Sundargarh and western Odisha. We believe that talent knows no boundaries, and every young mind deserves access to world-class learning facilities and modern vocational pathways.

How to Apply in 4 Simple Steps

Follow the streamlined process for direct institutional seats and SAMS Odisha counseling allotments.

1
Eligibility Check

Ensure qualification: 10th pass for 3-Year Diploma; 12th pass for BBA; Any Graduation for 2-Year MBA.

2
Register / Apply

Fill out the online admission form or register through D.T.E&T (SAMS Odisha) selecting BIT Bargaon.

3
Document Verification

Submit 10th/12th marksheets, conduct certificate, caste certificate (if applicable), and passport photos.

4
Confirm Admission

Pay nominal admission fees as per Govt. norms, receive student registration ID, and apply for scholarships.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions — BIT Bargaon, Sundargarh

Quick answers to common questions about admissions, diploma engineering branches, AICTE approvals, scholarships, and campus facilities at Bargaon Institute of Technology.

Which is the best diploma college in Sundargarh, Odisha?

Bargaon Institute of Technology (BIT) is widely recognized as the premier diploma engineering college in Sundargarh district, Odisha. Approved by AICTE, New Delhi and affiliated to SCTE&VT, Govt. of Odisha, BIT offers cutting-edge 3-Year Diploma programs in Mining, Mechanical, AIML, Electrical, and Civil Engineering with modern laboratories and 100% placement training.

What diploma engineering courses are offered in Sundargarh at BIT Bargaon?

BIT Bargaon offers 5 high-demand 3-Year Diploma in Engineering branches:

  • Diploma in Mining Engineering (60 Seats) — Hands-on geology and mine safety labs.
  • Diploma in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AIML) (60 Seats) — High-tech computer labs.
  • Diploma in Mechanical Engineering (120 Seats) — Workshop, CAD/CAM & Automobile labs.
  • Diploma in Electrical Engineering (60 Seats) — Power systems & machines lab.
  • Diploma in Civil Engineering (60 Seats) — Surveying, structural & concrete testing labs.
How to get admission in diploma engineering in Sundargarh through SAMS Odisha?

Admissions for 1st Year Diploma (for 10th pass students) and 2nd Year Lateral Entry (for +2 Science PCM or ITI pass students) are conducted through the official SAMS Odisha portal (skill.samsodisha.gov.in). You can also visit the BIT Bargaon admission cell directly for on-spot application assistance, counseling, and document verification.

Why is BIT Bargaon the top choice for Mining Engineering Diploma in Odisha?

Sundargarh is located at the center of the mining and metals belt of Eastern India. At BIT Bargaon, Mining Engineering students gain direct industry exposure through mine visits, open-cast and underground mining simulations, rock mechanics testing, and active placement relationships with leading mining and mineral enterprises.

Can diploma students avail Odisha State Scholarships at BIT Bargaon?

Yes. All eligible students from SC, ST, OBC, SEBC, General-EWS, and construction workers' children can avail 100% financial scholarship assistance through the Odisha State Scholarship Portal (scholarship.odisha.gov.in). Our scholarship welfare desk guides students through every step of the online application.

Are hostel and transport facilities available for students across Sundargarh?

Yes! BIT Bargaon provides dedicated, secure on-campus hostels for boys and girls with 24/7 security, high-speed Wi-Fi, nutritious mess food, and power backup. Furthermore, institute bus transport connects the campus with key hubs across Sundargarh district.
