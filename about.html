About Bargaon Institute of Technology

Pioneering industry-driven polytechnic and management education in Sundargarh district since inception.

About The Institution

Nurturing Technical Excellence in Sundargarh

Established with a visionary goal to empower Western Odisha through accessible, high-standard technical education, Bargaon Institute of Technology (BIT) stands as a beacon of academic rigor and industry-aligned training.

🏛️

Institutional Overview

BIT Bargaon is situated on an expansive, lush green campus in Sundargarh district, Odisha. Equipped with state-of-the-art laboratory infrastructure, modernized workshops, and dedicated faculty mentors, we prepare students for thriving careers in core engineering sectors.

  • 150-Acre peaceful academic environment
  • Accredited engineering faculty and mentors
  • Focus on rural empowerment & industry skill sets
🎯

Vision & Core Mission

To emerge as a premier polytechnic and management institute fostering innovation, industrial competency, ethical leadership, and technological solutions tailored to regional and national growth.

  • Experiential learning and hands-on workshops
  • Close partnerships with mineral and industrial hubs
  • Holistic personality and professional development
📜

Statutory Accreditations

Every program at BIT complies strictly with statutory guidelines from apex educational bodies, ensuring seamless credit recognition, state scholarship eligibility, and government job qualification.

  • Approved by AICTE, New Delhi
  • Affiliated to SCTE&VT, Govt. of Odisha
  • Affiliated to BPUT Odisha (Management)
  • Recognized by DTE&T Odisha
Leadership & Governance
