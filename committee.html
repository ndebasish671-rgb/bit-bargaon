Institutional Committees & Mandatory Disclosures

Anti-Ragging Committee & Squad

AICTE Mandatory Constitution

Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited on campus, in college buses, or during institutional tours. The committee enforces zero tolerance through round-the-clock flying squads.

  • Anti-Ragging National Toll-Free: 1800-180-5522
  • Institute Anti-Ragging Helpline: +91-7327078543
  • Mandatory student & parent online affidavits

Complaint & Grievance Redressal Committee

Student Grievance Cell

Provides a confidential platform for students and staff to submit any grievances regarding academics, evaluations, facilities, or administration.

  • Headed by senior professors and academic coordinators
  • Time-bound inquiry and documented resolutions
  • Confidential email: bitbargaon2026@gmail.com
👩‍💼

Internal Complaint Committee (Women Cell)

Gender Sensitization

Constitutes female faculty members and counselors to guarantee a safe, supportive, and dignified atmosphere for female students, staff, and visitors.

  • Workplace & campus harassment prevention
  • Gender equality and self-defense awareness drives
  • Confidential counseling support
📜

SC & ST Committee

Prevention of Atrocities

Formed to ensure the social welfare, equal opportunities, and academic inclusion of Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe students.

  • Monitoring of post-matric scholarship claims
  • Zero tolerance against caste discrimination
  • Special remedial mentoring classes
📄

RTI Cell

Right to Information

Formed in compliance with Right to Information provisions to ensure institutional transparency and provide timely replies to statutory information queries.

  • Designated Public Information Officer (PIO)
  • Appellate Authority for institutional disclosures
